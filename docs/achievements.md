# Cell Pet: silly achievements

The silly achievement lines, one pool per stage. Edit them here, then run `python -I tools/make_quips.py`. The tool keeps the section names and the event labels below as they are, so change the lines, not the headings.

**How they show:**
- A pop-up banner with a sparkle appears over the pond for about 6 s, at most two lines of 38 characters.
- They come at random times, 3 to 45 minutes apart (uniform).
- They keep coming while evolving is paused, since they are just for fun.
- In desk clock mode they sit under the big time.

**How lines are picked:**
- Each line comes from a big pool for the current stage. Round one has its own pool.
- No line repeats until its pool is used up; then the pool reshuffles.
- The position in the pool is saved, so a reboot doesn't repeat lines.
- A few lines fire on real events instead of the timer, once per pet.
